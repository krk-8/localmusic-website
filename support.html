What Is LocalMusic?

LocalMusic is an offline audio player for user-provided music files. You import songs from the Files app, organize them into a library and playlists, and play them back on your iPhone.

Main Features

  • import songs from the iOS Files picker
  • local offline playback without an internet connection
  • search and sorting in your library
  • edit song titles and artist names
  • create and manage playlists
  • background playback
  • lock screen and Control Center controls
  • Home Screen widget support
  • equalizer presets and custom tuning
  • Pro upgrade options (Yearly subscription or Lifetime unlock)

How Do I Import Songs?

  1. Open the Library tab.
  2. Tap the + button.
  3. Choose supported audio files from the iOS Files picker.
  4. LocalMusic imports the files into the app and adds them to your library.

Which Audio Formats Are Supported?

LocalMusic is designed for common local audio formats such as MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, AIFC, CAF, and AAC, subject to playback support available through iOS.

If a file does not import or play, it may be unsupported, corrupted, or encoded with a format that iOS cannot decode for playback.

Does LocalMusic Stream Music?

No. LocalMusic does not provide a streaming catalog. It plays audio files that you choose to import.

Are My Songs Uploaded Anywhere?

No. Imported songs are stored locally on your device by the app for playback and organization.

What Does Pro Unlock?

The free version lets you import up to 5 songs so you can test all playback features. Upgrading to Pro removes that limit and allows unlimited song imports.

What Plans Are Available For Pro?

LocalMusic offers two flexible purchase options to unlock Pro features:

  • Pro Yearly Subscription ($19.99 / year): Unlimited song imports billed annually through your Apple ID account. Automatically renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours prior to the end of the billing period.
  • Pro Lifetime Access ($9.99 one-time): A single one-time in-app purchase that provides permanent unlimited imports with no recurring charges.

How Do I Manage Or Cancel My Subscription?

If you subscribe to the Yearly plan, you can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your iPhone's Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions, or through your App Store account settings.

How Do I Restore My Purchase?

Open the Pro upgrade screen or Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Your purchase will be restored automatically if you are signed in with the same Apple ID used to make the original purchase.

Is Family Sharing Supported?

Check your App Store purchase details for the current availability of Family Sharing for LocalMusic Pro.

Why Is A Song Missing?

Possible reasons include:

  • the file was deleted from the app's local storage
  • the import failed or was interrupted
  • the file was unsupported or corrupted

Try importing the file again from the Files app.

Why Does A Song Show The Wrong Name Or Artist?

LocalMusic reads metadata from the audio file when available. You can edit the visible title and artist in the app after import.

Does LocalMusic Work Offline?

Yes. Once songs are imported, LocalMusic is completely offline and does not require an active internet connection for playback.

How Do I Create A Playlist?

  1. Open the Playlists tab.
  2. Create a new playlist.
  3. Add songs from your library.
  4. Reorder songs as needed.

Troubleshooting

If something is not working:

  • fully close and reopen the app
  • confirm your file format is supported
  • try importing the file again
  • make sure your iPhone has enough free storage
  • make sure your App Store purchase is signed in with the correct Apple ID
