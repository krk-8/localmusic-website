Pure Offline Utility • Bring Your Own Music

LocalMusic is strictly an offline software player and metadata management utility. LocalMusic does not provide, host, stream, scrape, index, or distribute any music or audio files. You supply 100% of the content you import. Users are solely responsible for ensuring they possess all necessary legal rights and authorizations for all imported audio files, lyrics, and artwork.
