Privacy Policy

Your data stays focused on your device.

LocalMusic is built with privacy at its core. Your imported songs and listening data remain strictly on your device.

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kanaiyalal Kavathiya handles information when you use the LocalMusic app.

1. Summary

LocalMusic is designed to play audio files that you choose to import. We do not provide a streaming music catalog, and we do not require you to create an account to use the app.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information directly through the app for our own analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes.

The app stores certain information locally on your device so it can function properly, including:

  • songs you choose to import into the app
  • song metadata such as title, artist, duration, and artwork
  • playlists you create
  • playback state such as queue, progress, shuffle, and repeat settings
  • equalizer settings
  • in-app purchase unlock state cached on-device

3. Files You Import

When you choose audio files through the iOS Files picker, LocalMusic may copy those files into the app's local storage so they can be played and managed inside the app.

These files are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers by the app.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the rights to use any audio files you import.

4. Purchases

If you purchase a Pro plan or unlock, payments are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details such as credit card numbers.

The app verifies your purchase entitlements through Apple's StoreKit framework and may cache your status locally on your device to ensure uninterrupted offline access.

5. Widgets And App Extensions

If you use LocalMusic widgets, the app may store limited now playing information in a shared app group container on your device so the widget can display current playback information and playback controls.

This may include:

  • current song title
  • current artist name
  • artwork associated with the current song
  • up next titles
  • playback state

This information is stored locally on your device for app functionality.

6. Tracking And Analytics

LocalMusic does not use advertising trackers in the app.

LocalMusic does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

7. Third-Party Services

The app relies on Apple services and frameworks for parts of its functionality, such as:

  • App Store and StoreKit for purchases and purchase restoration
  • iOS system media controls and lock screen now playing features
  • iOS Files picker for user-selected imports

These services are governed by Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

8. Data Retention

Because LocalMusic stores data locally on your device, imported songs, playlists, settings, and playback state remain on your device until you delete them, delete the app, or clear them yourself.

9. Children's Privacy

LocalMusic does not collect personal information from children or any other users.

10. International Users

If you use the app outside India, you understand that information handled by Apple services may be processed according to Apple's systems and policies.

11. Your Choices & Data Control

  • delete imported songs from within the app at any time
  • remove the app and all associated local data from your device
  • manage subscriptions and purchases through your Apple ID settings
  • contact us directly with any privacy-related questions

12. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the Last updated date above.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact kanaiyalalkavathiya@gmail.com or visit [SUPPORT URL].
