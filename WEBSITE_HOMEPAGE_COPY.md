## Feature Cards
1. **Import Files & Full Folders:** Import single tracks, multi-track selections, or complete album folders directly from iCloud Drive, On My iPhone, or connected USB drives. Plays MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, AIFF, and CAF.
2. **Real-Time Synced Lyrics:** Import synchronized `.lrc` files or view embedded ID3 lyrics with fluid, auto-scrolling timing that lets you follow along with every word.
3. **10-Band Graphic Equalizer:** Shape your sound with 10 frequency bands (32Hz to 16kHz) and instant audio presets including Bass Boost, Acoustic, Vocal, Treble Boost, Rock, and Pop.
4. **Smooth Crossfade Mixing:** Eliminate awkward silence between songs. Customize crossfade duration to smoothly blend consecutive tracks together like a continuous mix.
5. **Metadata & Cover Art Editor:** Correct missing tags directly in the app. Edit song titles, artists, and album names, or assign custom high-resolution cover artwork from your photo library.
6. **Private On-Device Stats:** Explore your personal listening habits: total hours listened, top tracks, favorite artists, and listening streaks—computed 100% locally with zero external tracking.
7. **Widgets & Lock Screen Controls:** Control playback from the Lock Screen and Control Center with full scrubbing and artwork, plus Home Screen widgets that keep your music a glance away.
8. **Sleep Timer & Siri Shortcuts:** Fall asleep to your favorite albums with an automatic shut-off sleep timer, or launch instant playback hands-free using Siri voice commands and iOS Shortcuts.
