## Short Value Proposition

LocalMusic is built for people who want a simple way to play personal audio files on iPhone without relying on a streaming subscription. Bring in your own songs, keep them on your device, and enjoy a focused offline listening experience.

## Key Features

### Import Your Own Files

Bring songs into the app using the iOS Files picker. LocalMusic supports common audio formats and extracts title, artist, duration, and artwork when available.

### Play Offline

Your music stays available on your device for offline playback. No streaming catalog required.

### Organize Your Library

Search your songs, sort by title, artist, or date added, edit song names, and remove tracks you no longer want in your library.

### Create Playlists

Build playlists for workouts, travel, study sessions, or anything else. Reorder tracks and start playback from any song.

### Smooth Playback Controls

Use the mini player, full now playing screen, shuffle, repeat, scrubber, and lock screen controls to manage playback quickly.

### Background Playback

Keep listening while using other apps, locking your phone, or switching tasks.

### Widget Support

See what is currently playing and access quick playback actions from your Home Screen.

### Equalizer

Adjust sound with a built-in equalizer and presets to match your listening style.

## Free And Pro

LocalMusic includes a free tier so you can test all features with up to 5 songs, with two flexible options to unlock unlimited imports:

- **Free:** import up to 5 songs, offline playback, playlists, widgets, and equalizer
- **Pro Yearly:** $19.99 / year auto-renewable subscription
- **Pro Lifetime:** $9.99 one-time purchase with no recurring fees
