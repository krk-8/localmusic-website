## 1. What Is LocalMusic?

LocalMusic is a modern, privacy-first offline audio player built specifically for iPhone listeners who own their music collection. Instead of relying on expensive streaming subscriptions with expiring licenses, LocalMusic lets you import and organize personal audio files with studio-grade sound controls, 100% offline.

## 2. Comprehensive Feature Overview

- **Folder & File Import:** Import single files, multiple tracks, or entire directory trees and albums recursively.
- **Broad Audio Codec Support:** Plays MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, AIFF, AIFC, CAF, and iOS CoreAudio formats.
- **10-Band Graphic Equalizer:** 32Hz to 16kHz precision sliders with built-in presets (Bass Boost, Acoustic, Vocal, Treble Boost, Rock, Pop, Flat, Custom).
- **Crossfade Audio Mixing:** Seamless track transitions with configurable crossfade duration.
- **Synced Lyrics Display:** Support for synchronized `.lrc` files, `.srt`, `.txt`, and embedded ID3 synchronized lyrics.
- **Metadata & Cover Art Editor:** Edit song titles, artist names, album titles, and assign custom cover artwork directly inside the app.
- **Dynamic Artwork Atmosphere:** Adaptive color glows in the player dynamically extracted from album artwork.
- **Private On-Device Listening Stats:** Tracks hours listened, top played songs, top artists, and listening streaks—computed locally with zero tracking.
- **Sleep Timer:** Auto-pause playback after 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes, or at the end of the current track.
- **Playback Modes & Queue:** Shuffle, Repeat (All / One / Off), variable playback speed, and interactive Up Next queue management.
- **System Integration:** Lock Screen and Control Center controls, CarPlay and Bluetooth headphone support, Home Screen widgets, and Siri Shortcuts.

## 3. How to Import Music

### Importing Single or Multiple Audio Files
1. Open the **Library** tab in LocalMusic.
2. Tap the **+** button in the top right.
3. Select **Import Files**.
4. Browse and select your audio files from iCloud Drive, the On My iPhone folder, or connected external storage.
5. Tap **Open** to import the files into your library.

### Importing Entire Music Folders & Albums
1. Tap the **+** button in the Library tab.
2. Select **Import Folder**.
3. Navigate to the folder containing your music (e.g., an artist or album directory).
4. Tap **Open**. LocalMusic will recursively scan and import all compatible audio tracks inside that directory.

## 4. How to Add and View Synced Lyrics

LocalMusic supports both embedded lyrics and standalone lyrics files:
- **Embedded Lyrics:** If your audio file contains embedded ID3 synchronized or unsynchronized lyrics, LocalMusic reads and displays them automatically.
- **Importing .LRC Files:** You can import standalone `.lrc`, `.srt`, or `.txt` files for any song via the song menu to enjoy real-time scrolling lyrics synced to the music.

## 5. 10-Band Equalizer & Crossfade Mixing

Open the Now Playing view and tap the equalizer icon to access the audio engine controls:
- **10-Band EQ:** Adjust frequencies across 32Hz, 64Hz, 125Hz, 250Hz, 500Hz, 1kHz, 2kHz, 4kHz, 8kHz, and 16kHz, or choose presets like *Bass Boost*, *Rock*, *Acoustic*, or *Vocal*.
- **Crossfade:** In Settings, configure crossfade duration to smoothly blend the end of one song into the beginning of the next, eliminating silent gaps between tracks.

## 6. Listening Stats & Privacy

LocalMusic includes an advanced Listening Stats tab that displays your total listening time, top tracks, favorite artists, and listening streaks. **All statistics are calculated and stored 100% locally on your iPhone using Core Data.** We never upload, transmit, or monetize your listening habits.

### Managing or Cancelling Subscriptions
Annual subscriptions are managed directly through Apple. You can review, change, or cancel your subscription anytime by opening your iPhone's **Settings > [Your Apple ID] > Subscriptions**.

### Restoring Purchases
If you switch to a new iPhone or reinstall the app, open Settings or the Pro upgrade sheet in LocalMusic and tap **Restore Purchases**. Your purchase will unlock immediately when signed in with the same Apple ID.

## 8. Copyright & Legal Compliance FAQ

### Does LocalMusic provide, host, or download music?
**No.** LocalMusic is an offline player software utility. We do not provide, host, stream, scrape, search, or distribute music or copyrighted recordings. You supply 100% of the audio files you import from your own personal collection.

### What are my responsibilities regarding music files?
You are solely responsible for ensuring you have the legal right, ownership, or authorized license to import and play any audio files, lyrics, or artwork you use in the app. Using LocalMusic to play unauthorized or pirated recordings violates our Terms of Use.

## 9. Hearing Safety & Volume Caution

Listening to loud audio through headphones or earbuds for extended periods can lead to permanent hearing loss or tinnitus. Use caution when boosting equalizer bands or raising volume levels. LocalMusic disclaims any liability for hearing impairment or equipment damage resulting from high-volume listening.

## 10. Backup Recommendations

Because LocalMusic stores imported songs locally in the app's sandboxed storage on your device, deleting the app will remove those local copies. Always maintain an independent backup of your music files on your computer, iCloud Drive, or external hard drive.

## 11. Troubleshooting & Bug Reports

If you encounter an issue:
- **File won't play:** Verify the audio format is supported (MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, AIFF, CAF). Highly proprietary or DRM-protected files (such as Apple Music streaming downloads) cannot be played by third-party apps.
- **App crashes or freezes:** Restart the app or restart your iPhone to free system memory.
- **Purchase not recognized:** Tap "Restore Purchases" in the app Settings while connected to the internet.
